CDC Health Alert: Increased Respiratory Illness Activity - Multiple States, 2024

Posted January 15, 2024

CDC is issuing this Health Alert Network (HAN) Health Alert to notify clinicians and public health officials about increased respiratory illness activity, particularly in the Pacific Northwest region including Idaho.

The Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) has detected increased respiratory illness activity across multiple states. This alert focuses on the Pacific Northwest region, with significant impacts reported in Idaho. Healthcare facilities are reporting a 15% increase in respiratory-related hospitalizations. Public health officials are advising increased vigilance and implementation of infection control measures.

Key Points:

  • 15% increase in respiratory hospitalizations in Idaho
  • Pediatric cases showing significant rise
  • Healthcare capacity concerns in affected regions
  • Source of increased transmission currently unknown

CDC Health Advisory: Measles Exposure in International Travelers

Posted January 10, 2024

CDC is issuing this Health Advisory to notify clinicians and public health officials about recent measles exposures in international travelers arriving in the United States.

Multiple cases of measles have been identified in travelers returning from regions with ongoing measles outbreaks. Public health departments are conducting contact tracing to identify potential exposures. Healthcare providers should maintain a high index of suspicion for measles in patients with compatible symptoms.

CDC Health Update: Novel Influenza A Virus Infections

Posted January 5, 2024

CDC is providing an update on novel influenza A virus infections reported in the United States.

Since the last update, three additional human infections with novel influenza A viruses have been reported. All cases occurred in people who had contact with infected swine. No human-to-human transmission has been identified. The risk to the general public remains low at this time.
