Current Disease Alerts and Advisories

Measles Exposure Advisory - Ada County

Issued: January 12, 2024

The Idaho Department of Health and Welfare is issuing this advisory regarding a confirmed case of measles in Ada County. The individual was exposed during international travel and may have exposed others in the community.

Key Information:

  • Location: Ada County, Idaho
  • Date of Exposure: January 8-10, 2024
  • Exposure Sites: Local hospital emergency department, grocery store, and community center
  • Current Status: Contact tracing underway, no additional cases identified at this time

Recommendations:

  • Individuals who were at the identified locations during the exposure period should monitor for symptoms
  • Healthcare providers should maintain a high index of suspicion for measles
  • Vaccination is the best protection against measles

Increased Respiratory Illness Activity

Issued: January 10, 2024

Idaho is experiencing increased respiratory illness activity, consistent with national trends. Healthcare facilities across the state are reporting elevated numbers of respiratory-related visits.

Current Data:

  • 15% increase in respiratory-related emergency department visits compared to same period last year
  • Pediatric cases showing the most significant increase
  • Multiple respiratory viruses circulating, including RSV, influenza, and COVID-19

Communicable Disease Information

Reportable Diseases

Idaho law requires healthcare providers and laboratories to report certain diseases and conditions to the Idaho Department of Health and Welfare. These reports help public health officials monitor disease trends and respond quickly to outbreaks.

Disease Surveillance

The Idaho Department of Health and Welfare conducts ongoing surveillance for communicable diseases to protect the health of Idaho residents. Surveillance activities include:

  • Monitoring disease trends and patterns
  • Investigating disease outbreaks
  • Identifying and responding to public health threats
  • Providing information and recommendations to healthcare providers and the public

Infection Prevention and Control

Infection prevention and control measures are essential for protecting individuals and communities from communicable diseases. The department provides guidance and resources for:

  • Healthcare settings
  • Schools and childcare facilities
  • Workplaces
  • Community organizations
