Disease Outbreak News (DON)

The Disease Outbreak News (DON) is a daily online service on outbreaks and other public health emergencies of international concern. It is managed by the WHO Health Emergencies Programme under the International Health Regulations (2005).

Current Outbreaks

Avian Influenza A(H5N1) - Europe

14 January 2024

Region: European Region

On 12 January 2024, the veterinary authorities of France reported a new outbreak of highly pathogenic avian influenza A(H5N1) in a commercial poultry farm in the Brittany region. This is part of an ongoing series of outbreaks in poultry and wild birds across Europe.

Since the beginning of December 2023, multiple European countries have reported outbreaks of highly pathogenic avian influenza (HPAI) A(H5N1) in poultry and wild birds. The affected countries include France, Germany, the Netherlands, and the United Kingdom. To date, no human infections with this virus have been reported in Europe.

Public Health Response:

  • Enhanced surveillance in affected and at-risk areas
  • Culling of infected and exposed poultry
  • Implementation of movement restrictions and biosecurity measures
  • Risk assessment for human health

WHO Risk Assessment:

The overall risk to the general population in Europe from this event is assessed as low. However, the risk assessment will be reviewed as new information becomes available. The risk to occupationally exposed persons (such as poultry workers) is assessed as low to moderate, depending on the implementation of protective measures.

Dengue Fever - South America

12 January 2024

Region: Region of the Americas

The Ministry of Health of Brazil has reported an increase in dengue fever cases in several states, with a notable rise in severe cases and deaths.

From 1 January to 10 January 2024, a total of 15 245 probable cases of dengue fever, including 12 deaths, have been reported in Brazil. The most affected states are São Paulo, Minas Gerais, and Rio de Janeiro. The circulating serotypes include DENV-1, DENV-2, and DENV-3.

Public Health Response:

  • Intensified vector control activities
  • Enhanced surveillance and case management
  • Community engagement and education campaigns
  • Coordination with neighboring countries

Marburg Virus Disease - Equatorial Guinea

10 January 2024

Region: African Region

The Ministry of Health and Social Welfare of Equatorial Guinea has officially declared an outbreak of Marburg virus disease in the country.

As of 10 January 2024, a total of 25 suspected cases, including nine deaths (case fatality ratio: 36%), have been reported from two provinces in Equatorial Guinea. This is the first time Marburg virus disease has been identified in the country.

Public Health Response:

  • Activation of the national emergency response committee
  • Deployment of rapid response teams to affected areas
  • Case investigation and contact tracing
  • Infection prevention and control measures in healthcare facilities
  • Cross-border surveillance and coordination

WHO Risk Assessment:

The risk at the national level is assessed as high due to the potential for further spread within the country, considering the novelty of the virus in Equatorial Guinea and the reported case fatality ratio. The risk at the regional level is assessed as moderate, and the risk at the global level is assessed as low.
